SIGNATURES

A signature drawn in Lithora is a picture placed in the document. It is not a
certified digital signature and does not by itself prove who signed a document.

PRIVACY

Lithora has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and collects no data about you or
your documents. It goes online only to check GitHub for a new version (which you can
turn off in Settings) and to download an update when you choose to install one.
